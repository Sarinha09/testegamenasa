02

RESOURCES & GRIDS

▼

MANUAL COLLECTION

Use Solar Panels and Water Generators to produce Energy and Water. Simply click on them whenever the ready indicator appears to collect resources.

STORAGE & AUTOMATION

Base storage is limited. Build Power Storage or Water Tanks to expand capacity and automate collection by linking them directly to your generators.

HOW TO LINK: Press C (or select the network tool on the HUD), click on a Solar Panel or Water Generator, and drag the mouse to connect a line straight to your storage unit.

03

HABITAT & RADIATION

▼

Your central Habitat consumes Food, Energy, Water, and Oxygen daily based on your crew size. Keep supplies balanced—if life support runs dry, the mission fails.

⚠ COSMIC RADIATION & UPGRADES

The Habitat shields astronauts from solar radiation, though never 100%. Accumulate required Energy and Water to upgrade your Habitat level—this boosts radiation shielding and unlocks buffs, but be careful: higher shielding increases daily maintenance costs!

FIG 2.0 — HABITAT SHIELDING & UPGRADE PANEL

04

COMMS & RANDOM EVENTS

▼

Keep an eye on the Communications Center inside your Habitat. When an alert pops up, click it to inspect incoming cosmic events before they hit.

You will have a short preparation window to stockpile resources or reinforce systems. Choose wisely—every decision carries real consequences for your crew's safety and survival.

05

MODULES & LUNAR CREDITS

▼

Each base module serves a specialized role and requires specific resources to operate. Expand your colony by purchasing new modules with Lunar Credits.

Earn Lunar Credits by completing mini-missions and resolving events. Balance your expansion carefully: more structures demand higher resource output!
